# Changelog

What changed in each version of Webco on the Chrome Web Store, newest first.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-22)

First release.

### Added

- An AI side panel next to any web page: ask in plain words about the page you're on, and it reads it and answers, showing each step as it goes. It reads, and changes nothing on any site.
- The first time it needs a site, Webco and then Chrome ask you to allow it. It reads the page in your own tab, so it sees what the page shows you.
- Add several open tabs to one question with @ or the + button, and compare or combine them.
- Read a link by its address, or search the web, with the sources named. Searches and pages rendered for you cost credits, or switch on "Search with DuckDuckGo" in Settings to search for free in a background tab.
- Ask about a YouTube video: it reads the video's transcript and chapters, and its answers point to the moments they come from.
- Select text on any page, right-click it, and pick "Ask Webco": the side panel opens with the text added to your question.
- Long pages are read in parts, and long chats are summarized so they can keep going.
- The same Cofun account and credits as Notico and Confluco: free credits every week, one-time packs through Stripe's checkout, and each answer shows what it cost. Or answer with your own model through OpenRouter.
- Usage data that never holds your messages, replies, or anything from your pages. Turn it off in Settings with "Share usage data".
