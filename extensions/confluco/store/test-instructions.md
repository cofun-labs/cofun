# Test instructions

For the store's reviewers. No test account is given: sign-up is open, and reviewers use a Confluence site of their own. The Dashboard's Additional instructions field takes at most 500 characters, so the text below stays under that.

```text
No test account is needed: sign-up is open and new accounts get free credits.

1. Click the Confluco icon: the side panel opens. Click "Sign in" (Google or an email code).
2. On any page, ask "Summarize this page." Allow the site when asked.
3. For Confluence, a free Confluence Cloud site works: open a page, allow the site, and ask "What does this page say?"

Pages change only when asked in an exact form, e.g. Replace "a" with "b" on this page.
```
