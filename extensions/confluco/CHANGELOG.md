# Changelog

What changed in each version of Confluco on the Chrome Web Store, newest first.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-19)

First release.

### Added

- An AI side panel next to Confluence: ask in plain words about the page you're on, and it reads, searches, and explains your pages, showing each step as it goes.
- Works on Confluence Cloud and on self-managed Confluence, in your own tab with the session you're signed in with. The first time on a site, Confluco and then Chrome ask you to allow it.
- Read the page you're on, search published content, browse spaces and page trees, and look into versions, access, labels, comments and replies, and attachments.
- Change pages when you ask in so many words: replace exact text, create a child page, rename a page, add or remove a label, post a footer comment or reply, move or copy a page within its space, move a page to the trash after you confirm, and restore a page. Undo the last verified edit or trash from the chat.
- Upload a file to the current page, or replace an attachment, after you confirm.
- Bring in the web: ask about any open tab, add several tabs to one question with @ or the + button, read a link, or search the web. Searches cost credits, or switch on "Search with DuckDuckGo" in Settings to search for free in a background tab.
- Select text on any page, right-click it, and pick "Ask Confluco": the side panel opens with the text added to your question.
- Long pages are read in parts, and long chats are summarized so they can keep going.
- Read only: switch the message box between Edit and Read only.
- The same Cofun account and credits as Notico: free credits every week, one-time packs through Stripe's checkout, and each answer shows what it cost. Or answer with your own model through OpenRouter.
- Usage data that never holds your messages, replies, or anything from your pages. Turn it off in Settings with "Share usage data".
